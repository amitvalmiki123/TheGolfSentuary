## Full Description

```text
MaxMusic — your music, without the noise.

Open the app, search any song, and it plays. No sign-up, no clutter — just full-length Hindi, Punjabi, Bollywood, 90s, Indie and Love songs in a clean dark design built for one-hand use on any phone.

Why people keep it installed:

• Find any song in seconds — search in Hindi or English, tap once to play
• Never lose the vibe — start a song from search and MaxMusic keeps the mood going with similar songs from that genre, instead of dropping into random results
• A queue you can actually follow — see what's next, and push any track to the front with Play next
• Saves your data — download songs and listen offline, on flights or with no network
• Your own music too — add MP3s from your phone in one tap
• Music that stays on — background playback with lock-screen, earbud and Bluetooth controls
• Sounds the way you like — bass, vocal and treble presets, plus a sleep timer for late nights
• Speaks your language — English and हिंदी interface

MaxMusic is a player only. It does not host or own any music; songs stream from publicly available sources and belong to their respective owners.
```
